# Fairtix design

The supplied guide is the design authority. Use printed-ticket layouts: paper #F3EFE6, card #FFFDF8, ink #16130F, muted ink #5B554C, rule #D9D2C3, orange #FF4A1C, valid #1F7A4D, blocked #C42B1C.

Archivo 700–800 for headings; IBM Plex Sans for body copy; IBM Plex Mono for dates and prices. Content uses a 1200px maximum and an 8px spacing rhythm. Ticket cards have a 1px ink border, 8px corners, a single 2px hard shadow, perforation and cut-outs. Rectangular buttons with 6px corners. Orange buttons use ink text. Orange text only at 24px or larger.

Show actual ticket interactions. No gradients, pills, glass, glow, decorative icon grids, artificial statistics, stock crowds, generated illustrations or vague diagrams. No invented clubs or testimonials. Example events are visibly labeled.

The latest user direction takes precedence over the original hero layout. Follow Jet HR's full home-page composition: an integrated header, pale lime background fading into white, condensed Mona Sans display type and a compact white action panel. The headline is “Tickets for your event, with a resale cap you control.” The hero contains no ticket. Its main action is “Try the demo”; the demo shows seller and buyer together. The example event is “Football match.” Other app screens retain the supplied paper, ink and orange palette.

Copy describes the action, price or rule. Avoid claims that scalping is impossible or rules can never change while upgrade authority exists. Technical details belong in verification drawers. Development scope belongs in About and the README, without repeated banners or explanatory labels in the main flow.
