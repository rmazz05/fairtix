# Fairtix design

The supplied guide is the design authority. Use printed-ticket layouts: paper #F3EFE6, card #FFFDF8, ink #16130F, muted ink #5B554C, rule #D9D2C3, orange #FF4A1C, valid #1F7A4D, blocked #C42B1C.

Archivo 700–800 for headings; IBM Plex Sans for body copy; IBM Plex Mono for dates and prices. Left aligned, 1200px maximum, an 8px spacing rhythm. Ticket cards have a 1px ink border, 8px corners, a single 2px hard shadow, perforation and cut-outs. Rectangular buttons with 6px corners. Orange buttons use ink text. Orange text only at 24px or larger.

Show actual ticket interactions. No gradients, pills, glass, glow, decorative icon grids, artificial statistics, stock crowds, generated illustrations or vague diagrams. No invented clubs or testimonials. Example events are visibly labeled.

Copy describes the action, price or rule. Headline: “Tickets with a resale cap.” Avoid claims that scalping is impossible or rules can never change while upgrade authority exists. Technical details belong in verification drawers. Test-credit status remains visible throughout checkout.
