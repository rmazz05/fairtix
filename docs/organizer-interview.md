## Message to adapt and send personally

Hi [name], I’m building Fairtix for the WHU Solana challenge. It lets an event organizer set a maximum resale price and receive a share of each resale.

The prototype uses test credits and does not admit anyone to an event. Could I show you a 90-second demo and ask how your club handles resales today?

If you think it could be useful, I’d like to quote your feedback in the pitch, with your permission. I’m also interested in what would have to work before you considered a pilot.

## Questions

- What happens when a buyer cannot attend? How often does that occur?
- Do you currently allow resale, refunds or ticket transfers?
- What resale price would you permit, and why?
- Would a resale royalty matter to your club? What would matter more?
- What must work at check-in before you would consider a trial?
