# Fairtix submission draft

Project name: Fairtix

Fairtix is a ticket app for student clubs and small venues. An organizer sets a maximum resale price and receives a royalty when a ticket is resold. A fan whose plans change can list their ticket within the cap. Finding a buyer is not guaranteed.

Solana is used for the ticket’s transfer rules. Each event issues Token-2022 general-admission tickets with a transfer hook. A direct transfer from an external wallet fails. The marketplace accepts only listings within the organizer’s cap and delivers the ticket with the seller, organizer and platform payments in one transaction.

The demo shows a €60 listing rejected against a €27.50 cap, a rejected direct transfer, and a completed capped resale. The first pilot target is a WHU student club. We would validate its resale volume and willingness to use a capped marketplace before expanding to small venues.

Limitations: test credits only, no entry/check-in, no mainnet. Cash side deals remain possible. The development upgrade authority is retained.
